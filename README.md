# Data Research & Analysis: Corporate Supply Chain & Public Procurement

**A data research and analysis project focused on corporate supply chain mapping and open-source data extraction.**

## 📌 Project Overview
This portfolio project demonstrates  skills in Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT), corporate compliance research, and data formatting. It focuses on extracting, verifying, and structuring complex B2B data—specifically government procurement contracts and Fortune 500 supply chain relationships.

## 📂 The Deliverable
* **Main File:** `Data_Research_and_Analysis_Project.xlsx`

## 🔍 Methodology & Scope

### Task 1: Government Procurement Data Extraction
* **Objective:** Extract and validate recent high-value government contracts awarded to global corporations across the EU, USA, and UK.
* **Sources:** Official government portals (SAM.gov, TED Europa, UK Contracts Finder).
* **Execution:** 
  * Standardized exact award amounts into pure numerical formats for database readiness.
  * Verified corporate entities, matching them with their correct main stock exchange, financial Ticker, and ISIN numbers.
  * Secured permanent, public-facing URLs to ensure data reproducibility, navigating around temporary session-based links.

### Task 2: Supply Chain & Compliance Mapping (P&G)
* **Objective:** Map the B2B supply chain ecosystem (Suppliers, Customers, Partners) for The Procter & Gamble Company.
* **Sources:** Primary corporate sources only (P&G Official Website, ESG/DEI Reports, SEC 10-K Filings).
* **Execution:** 
  * Successfully mapped over 30 verified corporate relationships.
  * Adhered to strict compliance guidelines: if specific relationship values or headquarters locations were not explicitly disclosed in the primary source, they were accurately labeled as "Not disclosed" rather than estimated via unverified third-party platforms.

## 🛠️ Key Skills Demonstrated
* **OSINT & Compliance Research:** Navigating complex databases and SEC filings.
* **Data Integrity & Cleaning:** Standardizing regional formatting, ensuring numerical accuracy, and eliminating dirty data.
* **Attention to Detail:** Strict adherence to sourcing and screening protocols.
